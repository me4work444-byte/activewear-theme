Privacy policy.

Your data belongs to you. Here's exactly how we handle it.

What we collect

  • Order details — name, shipping address, email, and what you bought
  • Payment details — processed by our encrypted payment providers; we never see or store card numbers
  • Basic usage — what pages you visit, so the site loads better for you

How we use it

To fulfil and deliver your orders, to send order and shipping updates, and to reply when you contact support. We don't sell your data. We don't rent your data. We don't share it with third parties for their advertising.

Your rights

  • Request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct or delete it
  • Opt out of marketing emails at any time — one click in any email

Email support@techactive.example any time and a human responds within hours.

Cookies

We use only essential cookies to keep the store working — nothing invasive, no cross-site tracking for ads.
