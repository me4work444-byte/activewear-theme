Shipping policy.

Fast, tracked, and on us.

Processing

Every order is packed and dispatched from our studio within 24–48 hours of purchase. You'll get a confirmation email the moment your parcel leaves — no guesswork.

Delivery times

  • Standard (free) — 3–5 business days, tracked to your door
  • Express — 1–3 business days at checkout
  • International — 5–12 business days depending on destination and customs

Tracking & duties

Every order ships with live tracking. Track it from the link in your confirmation email, updated at every checkpoint.

For international orders, import duties and taxes are calculated at checkout where supported, or collected by the carrier on delivery. We label shipments transparently and never inflate customs values.
