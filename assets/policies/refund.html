Refund policy.

Love your gear or get every cent back. That's the deal — in plain words.

60-day money-back guarantee

If you're not happy with your TechActive gear for any reason — or no reason at all — you can return it within 60 days of delivery for a full refund to your original payment method. No questions, no restocking fees, no return-shipping charge.

What we cover

  • Full refund on the purchase price within the 60-day window
  • Free return shipping via your prepaid label from the confirmation email
  • Free size exchanges — we'll even send the new size before you send the old one back

1-year seam guarantee

If the seams fatigue or the knit compromises from honest use (washing, training, ordinary wear) within one year of purchase, we replace the piece free of charge.

How to start a return

  • Reply to your order confirmation email with "Return" and your order number
  • Or email support@techactive.example — a human replies within hours
  • Use the prepaid label, drop the parcel off, and you're done

Refunds are processed within 3–5 business days of the return arriving at our studio and appear back on your original payment method.
